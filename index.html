      A COMMUNITY FIELD GUIDE

      One more campaign.

      Kaiserreich Run Picker helps you choose a country and political path for Hearts of Iron IV’s Kaiserreich mod, and remember the campaigns you have played.

      A growing campaign atlas

      The starting-country roster is complete for the pinned source snapshot. Political paths are incomplete: each nation is labelled with its reviewed route count or Paths pending. Later civil-war splinters and releasable nations are listed separately with availability labels. Each path links to the official source in the checklist. Some paths require unification or later elections; read the result notes before starting.

      How the draw works

      Fully random and Randomize run give every eligible country–path combination an equal chance. Spin country includes nations awaiting path review and chooses equally among eligible countries; Spin ideology chooses equally among that country’s eligible ideology groups. Spin path then chooses equally within the selected group. Ideology → Country uses your selected ideology and draws equally among matching path records.

      Your progress, your browser

      Start run marks a path Played. Completed always requires your choice. No accounts, cookies, analytics or external database are used by this application. Progress stays in localStorage on this browser and website address, unless you export it. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove it.

      Independent and open source

      An unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the Kaiserreich development team or Paradox Interactive. Flags and ideology emblems come from the official Kaiserreich source; they remain the property of their respective creators and are not covered by this app’s MIT code license. Flags follow confirmed path overrides or available ideology variants, then fall back to the country reference flag. Conditional in-game flag changes may differ.
